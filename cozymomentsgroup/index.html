About

We find the creators people already want to be around — then we build around them.

Cozy Moments Group discovers and spotlights aesthetically aligned creators in the luxury, lifestyle, travel, beauty, wellness and fashion spaces. We help them shape a personal brand that holds up, secure the partnerships and press that fit it, and — as the relationship grows — manage the whole thing.

For brands and events, we’re the shortlist: a curated group of creators whose audience, taste and content are already where you want to be.
